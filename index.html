Reconstruction

A Blind Benchmark for Recovering Research Ideas from Pre-Publication Bibliographies

S. Chen, Y. Fei, Nazhou Liu, X. Yu, L. Li, R. Thapa, M. Ciobanu, Q. Mao, R. Das

A blind benchmark that asks models to recover a paper's research idea from only its pre-publication bibliography. Across 643 papers in six domains, a multi-agent pipeline with cross-model review lifts Match rates about 2.4× over the best single model.
